### 2️⃣ SQL EDA & Advanced Analytics

![MySQL](https://img.shields.io/badge/MySQL_8.0-4479A1?style=flat-square&logo=mysql&logoColor=white)
![SQL](https://img.shields.io/badge/SQL-336791?style=flat-square)
![Focus](https://img.shields.io/badge/EDA_·_Advanced_Analytics-0B2A3F?style=flat-square)

Exploratory data analysis and advanced analytics in **MySQL 8.0**, built on the **Gold layer** of project 1 (customers, products, and sales transactions). It measures business performance, identifies trends, and produces KPI-ready reports.

- 🔍 **Exploration and magnitude:** dimensions, date ranges, and key business measures
- 🏆 **Ranking and performance:** top and bottom products and customers, plus period comparisons with window functions
- 📈 **Trends:** change over time and cumulative running totals
- 🧩 **Part-to-whole and segmentation:** category contribution, product cost segments, and customer segments
- 📋 **Reports:** `gold_report_customers` and `gold_report_products` views with KPIs such as recency, lifespan, and average order value

| Total Sales | Total Orders | Customers | Products | Period |
|:-----------:|:------------:|:---------:|:--------:|:------:|

> 🔗 Projects 1 and 2 form an **end-to-end data workflow**: preparing reliable datasets, then turning them into meaningful business information.
